LYRA VOCAL SYNTH

Voice from physics

A complete, interactive, musical TTS built on a real-time physical model of the airway. No samples. Pure simulation.

What Lyra is

Every sound is born from a simulated airway — lungs, vocal folds or syrinx, branched vocal tract, lips and nose. The same engine that runs in the browser also powers offline rendering and future studio tools.

Capabilities

Realistic TTS

High-quality G2P, coarticulation and prosody. English first, multi-language later.

Musical Singing

Piano-roll, lyrics per note, expressive timing, vibrato and dynamics.

Interactive Play

Drag tongue, lips and jaw on a live anatomical model. Real-time formants.

Beatbox & Gestures

Every percussion sound is an articulatory gesture — clicks, ejectives, bursts.

Bestiary

Human continuous morph + songbird, mammals and more exotic models.

Studio Power

MIDI, multi-voice, spectrum, formant tracking, WAV export, future plugins.

Why physical modeling?

Concatenative and neural systems stitch or statistically generate speech. Lyra computes how air moves through a changing tube. This gives continuous, controllable, explorable timbres — and makes non-human and impossible voices first-class citizens of the instrument.

Tech

Rust core

Single high-performance engine for browser (WASM + AudioWorklet) and native CLI.

FDTD airway

Branched staggered-grid transmission line with losses, pressure and moving walls.

Modern web UI

Cinematic dark design, 3D head, live anatomy, piano roll and spectrum views.
