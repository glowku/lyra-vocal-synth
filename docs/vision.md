# Product Vision — Lyra

Lyra is not just another TTS.

It is a **complete musical instrument** whose sound is generated by the physics of a living (or imaginary) vocal system.

## Core promises

- **Realistic** — continuous coarticulation, natural prosody, believable breath and effort.
- **Musical** — sing with expression, control vibrato, dynamics, phrasing, emotion.
- **Interactive** — drag the tongue in real time and hear the formants move; play MIDI; step-sequence beatbox gestures.
- **Powerful** — multi-voice, bestiary, studio export, research tools, future plugins.
- **Beautiful** — the interface itself is part of the experience: cinematic dark UI, live 3D head, glowing pressure fields, vector anatomy.

## Target users

1. Musicians & producers who want a vocal instrument that is not sample-based.
2. Researchers in speech and singing synthesis.
3. Developers building expressive voice applications.
4. Artists exploring non-human and hybrid voices.

## Differentiation

Unlike concatenative or neural TTS, Lyra never stitches recordings.  
Unlike classic formant synths, it models the actual tube and sources.  
Unlike pure research tools, it is designed from day one as a playable, beautiful instrument.

## Long-term dream

A single engine that can:
- Speak any language with high naturalness
- Sing with the control of a great vocalist
- Become a bird, a seal, an alien, or a choir
- Live inside a DAW, a game, a browser, and a research notebook

All from the same physical model.
